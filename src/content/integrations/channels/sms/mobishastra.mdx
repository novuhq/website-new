<IntegrationSection title="Overview" icon="eye">
  Mobishastra is a cloud communications provider for sending SMS and other
  customer messages.
</IntegrationSection>

<IntegrationSection title="How it works" icon="refresh-cw">
  Novu sends the content from your SMS workflow step through Mobishastra to the
  phone number stored on the subscriber profile.
</IntegrationSection>

<IntegrationSection title="Configure" icon="settings">
  Add the Mobishastra account credentials and sender value requested by the
  integration form in Novu.
</IntegrationSection>
