<IntegrationSection title="Overview" icon="eye">
  RingCentral is a cloud communications platform with messaging, phone, video,
  and contact-center services.
</IntegrationSection>

<IntegrationSection title="How it works" icon="refresh-cw">
  Novu sends the content from your SMS workflow step through the RingCentral
  messaging API to the subscriber's phone number.
</IntegrationSection>

<IntegrationSection title="Configure" icon="settings">
  Add the RingCentral application credentials and sender number requested by the
  integration form in Novu.
</IntegrationSection>
