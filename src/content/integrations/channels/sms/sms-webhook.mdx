<IntegrationSection title="Overview" icon="eye">
  SMS Webhook is a flexible provider for connecting Novu to an SMS service or
  internal delivery system that does not have a built-in integration.
</IntegrationSection>

<IntegrationSection title="How it works" icon="refresh-cw">
  When an SMS step runs, Novu sends the recipient, sender, message content, and
  related delivery data to your configured webhook endpoint. Your service can
  then transform or forward the request to the SMS gateway you use.
</IntegrationSection>

<IntegrationSection title="Configure" icon="settings">
  Enter the webhook URL and the authentication values required by your endpoint.
  Ensure the endpoint accepts the request format sent by Novu and returns a
  successful response after processing the message.
</IntegrationSection>
