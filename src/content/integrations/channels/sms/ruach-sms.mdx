<IntegrationSection title="Overview" icon="eye">
  Ruach SMS is an SMS provider for delivering text messages to customers and
  subscribers.
</IntegrationSection>

<IntegrationSection title="How it works" icon="refresh-cw">
  Novu sends the content from your SMS workflow step to Ruach SMS for delivery
  to the subscriber's phone number.
</IntegrationSection>

<IntegrationSection title="Configure" icon="settings">
  Add the Ruach SMS credentials and sender ID requested by the integration form
  in Novu.
</IntegrationSection>
