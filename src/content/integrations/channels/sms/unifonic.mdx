<IntegrationSection title="Overview" icon="eye">
  Unifonic is a customer engagement platform that provides APIs for SMS and
  other communication channels.
</IntegrationSection>

<IntegrationSection title="How it works" icon="refresh-cw">
  Novu sends the content from your SMS workflow step through Unifonic to the
  phone number stored on the subscriber profile.
</IntegrationSection>

<IntegrationSection title="Configure" icon="settings">
  Add your Unifonic App SID and the sender ID used for outgoing SMS messages.
</IntegrationSection>
