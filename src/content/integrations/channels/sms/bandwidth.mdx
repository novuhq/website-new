<IntegrationSection title="Overview" icon="eye">
  Bandwidth is a cloud communications platform that provides APIs for sending
  SMS and other customer communications.
</IntegrationSection>

<IntegrationSection title="How it works" icon="refresh-cw">
  Novu sends the content from your SMS workflow step through Bandwidth to the
  phone number stored on the subscriber profile.
</IntegrationSection>

<IntegrationSection title="Configure" icon="settings">
  Add the Bandwidth account credentials and sender value requested by the
  integration form in Novu.
</IntegrationSection>
