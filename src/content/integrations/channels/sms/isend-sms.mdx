<IntegrationSection title="Overview" icon="eye">
  iSend SMS is an SMS provider for delivering transactional and customer
  communication messages.
</IntegrationSection>

<IntegrationSection title="How it works" icon="refresh-cw">
  Novu sends the content from your SMS workflow step through iSend SMS to the
  phone number stored on the subscriber profile.
</IntegrationSection>

<IntegrationSection title="Configure" icon="settings">
  Add the iSend SMS credentials and sender value requested by the integration
  form in Novu.
</IntegrationSection>
