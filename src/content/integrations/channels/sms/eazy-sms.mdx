<IntegrationSection title="Overview" icon="eye">
  Eazy is an SMS delivery provider for sending text messages to customers
  and subscribers.
</IntegrationSection>

<IntegrationSection title="How it works" icon="refresh-cw">
  Novu sends the content from your SMS workflow step to Eazy for delivery to
  the subscriber's phone number.
</IntegrationSection>

<IntegrationSection title="Configure" icon="settings">
  Add the Eazy credentials and sender value requested by the integration
  form in Novu.
</IntegrationSection>
