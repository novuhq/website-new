<IntegrationSection title="Overview" icon="eye">
  Brevo is a customer communication platform that supports transactional email,
  SMS, and other messaging capabilities.
</IntegrationSection>

<IntegrationSection title="How it works" icon="refresh-cw">
  Novu sends the content from your SMS workflow step to Brevo for delivery to
  the phone number stored on the subscriber profile.
</IntegrationSection>

<IntegrationSection title="Configure" icon="settings">
  Add your Brevo API key and the sender name or number configured for your Brevo
  SMS account.
</IntegrationSection>
