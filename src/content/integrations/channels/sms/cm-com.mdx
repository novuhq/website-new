<IntegrationSection title="Overview" icon="eye">
  CM.com is a cloud communications platform that provides APIs for SMS and other
  customer communication channels.
</IntegrationSection>

<IntegrationSection title="How it works" icon="refresh-cw">
  Novu sends the content from your SMS workflow step through CM.com to the
  subscriber's phone number.
</IntegrationSection>

<IntegrationSection title="Configure" icon="settings">
  Add your CM.com product token and the sender value used for outgoing SMS
  messages.
</IntegrationSection>
