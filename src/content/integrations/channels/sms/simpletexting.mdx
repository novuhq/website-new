<IntegrationSection title="Overview" icon="eye">
  SimpleTexting is an SMS platform for sending text messages to customers and
  subscribers.
</IntegrationSection>

<IntegrationSection title="How it works" icon="refresh-cw">
  Novu sends the content from your SMS workflow step to SimpleTexting, which
  delivers the message to the phone number stored on the subscriber profile.
</IntegrationSection>

<IntegrationSection title="Configure" icon="settings">
  Add your SimpleTexting API key and the phone number or sender value used for
  outgoing messages.
</IntegrationSection>
