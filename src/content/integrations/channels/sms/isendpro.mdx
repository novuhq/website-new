<IntegrationSection title="Overview" icon="eye">
  iSendPro is an SMS platform for delivering transactional and customer
  communication messages.
</IntegrationSection>

<IntegrationSection title="How it works" icon="refresh-cw">
  Novu sends the content from your SMS workflow step to iSendPro for delivery to
  the phone number stored on the subscriber profile.
</IntegrationSection>

<IntegrationSection title="Configure" icon="settings">
  Add your iSendPro API credentials and the sender value used for outgoing SMS
  messages.
</IntegrationSection>
