<IntegrationSection title="Overview" icon="eye">
  iMedia is an SMS delivery provider for sending text messages to customers and
  subscribers.
</IntegrationSection>

<IntegrationSection title="How it works" icon="refresh-cw">
  Novu sends the content from your SMS workflow step to iMedia for delivery to
  the subscriber's phone number.
</IntegrationSection>

<IntegrationSection title="Configure" icon="settings">
  Add the iMedia credentials and sender value requested by the integration form
  in Novu.
</IntegrationSection>
