<IntegrationSection title="Overview" icon="eye">
  SMSMode is an SMS platform for sending transactional and customer
  communication messages.
</IntegrationSection>

<IntegrationSection title="How it works" icon="refresh-cw">
  Novu sends the content from your SMS workflow step to SMSMode for delivery to
  the subscriber's phone number.
</IntegrationSection>

<IntegrationSection title="Configure" icon="settings">
  Add your SMSMode API key and the sender value used for outgoing messages.
</IntegrationSection>
