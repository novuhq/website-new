<IntegrationSection title="Overview" icon="eye">
  LINE is a messaging platform for direct and business communication with users.
</IntegrationSection>

<IntegrationSection title="How it works" icon="refresh-cw">
  Novu sends the content from your chat workflow step through the LINE Messaging
  API to the user associated with the subscriber's channel credentials.
</IntegrationSection>

<IntegrationSection title="Configure" icon="settings">
  Add your LINE channel access token to Novu, then store the destination user ID
  in the subscriber's channel credentials.
</IntegrationSection>
