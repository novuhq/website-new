<IntegrationSection title="Overview" icon="eye">
  Telegram is a messaging platform that supports bots, direct conversations, and
  group chats.
</IntegrationSection>

<IntegrationSection title="How it works" icon="refresh-cw">
  Novu sends the content from your chat workflow step through a Telegram bot to
  the chat associated with the subscriber's channel credentials.
</IntegrationSection>

<IntegrationSection title="Configure" icon="settings">
  Create a bot with BotFather, add the bot token to the Telegram integration in
  Novu, and store the destination chat ID for each subscriber.
</IntegrationSection>
