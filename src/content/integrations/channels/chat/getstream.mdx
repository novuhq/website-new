<IntegrationSection title="Overview" icon="eye">
  GetStream provides APIs and SDKs for adding chat and activity feeds to
  applications.
</IntegrationSection>

<IntegrationSection title="How it works" icon="refresh-cw">
  Novu sends the content from your chat workflow step through GetStream to the
  channel or user associated with the subscriber's credentials.
</IntegrationSection>

<IntegrationSection title="Configure" icon="settings">
  Add your GetStream application credentials to Novu, then store the target
  channel or user information for each subscriber.
</IntegrationSection>
