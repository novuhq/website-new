<IntegrationSection title="Overview" icon="eye">
  Webex Messaging is a collaboration platform for team and direct conversations
  within Webex.
</IntegrationSection>

<IntegrationSection title="How it works" icon="refresh-cw">
  Novu sends the content from your chat workflow step through Webex Messaging to
  the destination associated with the subscriber's channel credentials.
</IntegrationSection>

<IntegrationSection title="Configure" icon="settings">
  Add the Webex credentials requested by the integration form, then store the
  target Webex destination for each subscriber.
</IntegrationSection>
