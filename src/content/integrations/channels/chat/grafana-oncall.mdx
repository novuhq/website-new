<IntegrationSection title="Overview" icon="eye">
  Grafana OnCall is an on-call management system for routing alerts and
  coordinating incident response.
</IntegrationSection>

<IntegrationSection title="How it works" icon="refresh-cw">
  Novu sends the content from your chat workflow step to a Grafana OnCall
  webhook URL, where it can be routed through your configured escalation and
  response workflows.
</IntegrationSection>

<IntegrationSection title="Configure" icon="settings">
  Create an incoming webhook in Grafana OnCall, add the Grafana OnCall provider
  in Novu, and store the webhook URL in the subscriber's channel credentials.
</IntegrationSection>
