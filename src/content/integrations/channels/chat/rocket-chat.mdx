<IntegrationSection title="Overview" icon="eye">
  Rocket.Chat is an open-source communication platform for team and customer
  conversations.
</IntegrationSection>

<IntegrationSection title="How it works" icon="refresh-cw">
  Novu sends the content from your chat workflow step to the Rocket.Chat webhook
  URL associated with the subscriber.
</IntegrationSection>

<IntegrationSection title="Configure" icon="settings">
  Create an incoming webhook in Rocket.Chat, add the Rocket.Chat provider in
  Novu, and store the webhook URL in the subscriber's channel credentials.
</IntegrationSection>
