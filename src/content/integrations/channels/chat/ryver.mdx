<IntegrationSection title="Overview" icon="eye">
  Ryver is a team collaboration platform that combines chat and task management.
</IntegrationSection>

<IntegrationSection title="How it works" icon="refresh-cw">
  Novu sends the content from your chat workflow step to the Ryver webhook URL
  stored for the subscriber.
</IntegrationSection>

<IntegrationSection title="Configure" icon="settings">
  Create an incoming webhook in Ryver, add the Ryver provider in Novu, and store
  the webhook URL in the subscriber's channel credentials.
</IntegrationSection>
