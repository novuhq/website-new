<IntegrationSection title="Overview" icon="eye">
  Microsoft Teams is a communication and collaboration platform used across
  Microsoft 365 organizations. Novu Connect lets your existing agent participate
  in Teams conversations without requiring a separate integration layer for
  Teams messages and events.
</IntegrationSection>

<IntegrationSection title="How it works" icon="refresh-cw">
  Novu Connect receives messages and replies from Microsoft Teams, preserves
  their conversation context, and routes them to your agent. Responses are
  delivered back to the correct conversation with support for Adaptive Cards,
  actions, approvals, rich text, reactions, ordered message processing, and
  messages that update in place.
</IntegrationSection>

<IntegrationSection title="Configure" icon="settings">
From your agent project, run:

`npx novu connect --channel teams`

Complete the CLI flow. When the CLI opens the Novu dashboard, finish the Microsoft Teams connection there and return to the CLI to complete the setup.

</IntegrationSection>
