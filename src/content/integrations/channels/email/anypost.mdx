<IntegrationSection title="Overview" icon="eye">
  Anypost is an email delivery platform for sending transactional and broadcast
  email through an HTTP API or SMTP.
</IntegrationSection>

<IntegrationSection title="How it works" icon="refresh-cw">
  Novu renders the content from your email workflow step and sends it to Anypost
  for delivery to the subscriber's email address.
</IntegrationSection>

<IntegrationSection title="Configure" icon="settings">
  Add your Anypost API key, authenticated sender email address, and sender name
  to the Anypost integration in Novu.
</IntegrationSection>
